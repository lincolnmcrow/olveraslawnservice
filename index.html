The Journey of a Young Entrepreneur

Olvera's Lawn Service is a proud Latino-owned business that started with one mower, one neighborhood, and a work ethic that wouldn't quit. What began as a way to earn extra money quickly turned into a real commitment to doing right by every yard and every customer.

Today that same drive shows up in every mow, every cleanup, and every conversation with a homeowner. Building this business one satisfied customer at a time is still the goal — and it's what keeps Olvera's Lawn Service showing up, rain or shine.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need to be home when you service my lawn?

No, you do not need to be home as long as our crew has clear, unobstructed access to your yard (unlocked gates, pets secured inside).

What happens if it rains on my scheduled service day?

Rain and wet turf can damage your lawn. In the event of inclement weather, our schedule shifts automatically by 1-2 days. Review our Weather & Cancellation Policy for complete details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards through secure electronic invoicing. All services are governed by our standard Terms of Service.

How is my personal information protected?

We respect your privacy and only use your details for scheduling and servicing your property. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
