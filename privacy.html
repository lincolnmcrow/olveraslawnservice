Privacy Policy

Olvera's Lawn Service values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website, estimate forms, phone calls, text messages, or other customer communications.

Information We Collect

We may collect information including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service property address
  • Billing information necessary to process payments
  • Yard size, condition, and service details
  • Gate codes or access instructions when voluntarily provided
  • Notes necessary to provide estimates and lawn maintenance services
  • Information customers voluntarily provide through quote or contact forms

Use of Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Provide estimates
  • Schedule lawn services
  • Perform requested services
  • Communicate service updates
  • Process payments
  • Send appointment or weather notifications
  • Maintain customer accounts
  • Respond to customer questions or requests
  • Improve our services and customer experience

We do not sell customer personal information to third-party marketers.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the business, such as payment processing, scheduling, website hosting, communication, or other business-support services.

Information Security

Olvera's Lawn Service takes reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no website, online form, or electronic communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through website forms.

Customer Privacy Requests

Customers may contact Olvera's Lawn Service to ask questions about the personal information they have provided or to request corrections to inaccurate contact information.

Olvera's Lawn Service reserves the right to update these Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Weather Policies when necessary. Updated policies will be posted on this website and may apply to future services. Customers are encouraged to review these policies periodically for changes.
